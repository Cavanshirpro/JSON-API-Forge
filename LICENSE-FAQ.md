# License FAQ

JSON API Forge uses the **JSON API Forge Source-Available Self-Host License 1.1** (`LicenseRef-JAF-SASH-1.1`). This is a plain-language summary; `LICENSE` controls if there is a conflict.

## Is it open source?
No in the OSI sense. Source is public for inspection, auditing, private modification, contribution and self-hosting, but redistribution and alternative distributions are restricted. Describe it as **source-available**.

## Can I self-host or use it commercially?
Yes. You may run it on infrastructure you control and use a privately hosted deployment commercially, subject to the license.

## Can I privately modify it?
Yes, for your own/private/internal deployment. Running a privately modified backend as a service is allowed when users do not receive a copy of the framework.

## Can I publish or send my copy or modified version?
Not without separate written permission, except for the narrow contribution-fork exception. Link third parties to the canonical repository or Official Release instead.

## Can I publish a community edition, fork, package, Docker image or rebrand?
Not without separate written authorization, whether free, paid, source-available, open-source or proprietary.

## Can I build a product on it?
You may operate a hosted product on top of Forge, but distributing a product containing a material copy of the framework requires separate authorization.

## Can ownership or licensing change later?
The owner may choose different terms for future releases and may lawfully transfer relevant rights to a company or successor. Existing valid licenses attached to already received copies are not silently rewritten.

## Why a CLA?
Contributors keep their copyright, while the CLA grants broad sublicensable, transferable and relicensable project rights needed for centralized release policy and a possible future company transfer.

For redistribution, OEM or alternative-version rights, obtain separate written permission from the Project Owner.
